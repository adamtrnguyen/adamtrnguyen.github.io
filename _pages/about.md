I'm an M.S. Computer Science student at the [University of Maryland](https://www.cs.umd.edu/) (expected May 2028), after a B.S. in Computer Science from Virginia Tech. From January to August 2027 I'll be at AMD as an AI Agentic/ML System Co-op.

My research interest is **evaluation and reliable systems**: empirical evaluation and measurement for single-agent and multi-agent systems. AI can now search the implementation space by brute force, so I'd like to understand what it means to measure and verify the result.

At the [ReDS Lab](https://reds-lab.github.io/) I worked with [Prof. Ruoxi Jia](https://ruoxijia.info/) on data selection, evaluation, and model memorization and safety. I'm a co-author on _Curation-Bench_ (Can Generalist Agents Automate Data Curation?, EMNLP 2026), which tests whether generalist coding agents can curate training data, with training and evaluation held fixed so that only the data changes, and on _Retracing the Past: LLMs Emit Training Data When They Get Lost_ (EMNLP 2025), which tests whether confusing an LLM makes it emit memorized training data.

On Virginia Tech's HokieTokie team in the [Amazon Nova AI Challenge](https://www.amazon.science/amazon-nova-ai-challenge-trusted-ai), I built SFT/DPO training infrastructure for an internal 8B code model and generated adversarial safety data to make it write safer code and refuse malicious requests, work published in the challenge proceedings. I also built and maintained ML training and evaluation infrastructure across VT's HPC and H100 clusters, and interned at Raytheon Technologies through the VT-Raytheon Fellowship.

In my free time I enjoy working on my Mac Studio, Mac mini, and UGREEN NAS homelab, building a RAG system, and reading philosophy.
